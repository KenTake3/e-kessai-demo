Approval Policy (excerpt) & Writing Examples

For the full text, see "Policies" on the intranet portal. This page is an excerpt.

Article 5 (Approval categories)

1. The applicant selects the approval category that matches the request.
2. Services agreements for implementing, developing, configuring or operating IT systems (software, cloud services, AI tools, etc.) must be filed as "Services Agreement (IT Systems)", regardless of contract type (deliverable-based or time & materials).
3. Of the services agreements in the preceding paragraph, those whose deliverables are capitalized as fixed assets (software) must be filed as "IT Investment (Capitalized Software)".
